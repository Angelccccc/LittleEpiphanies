On being alone

There are moments when I realize that no one will ever completely understand me.

Not because they don't care. Not because they aren't listening. But because there is an entire interior world inside every person that can never be fully translated into words.

And somehow, that thought is both terrifying and comforting.

Maybe being understood was never supposed to mean being understood completely.

Maybe it just means finding someone willing to keep trying.
