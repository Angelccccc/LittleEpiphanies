new starts

Do you ever get this feeling that you are a sheep following the crowd? Do you ever get this feeling that you have no agency and in reality life is set out before you, swept by the tides of society and culture, swept by the ways of the world. And most of us follow blindly. We study hard, get into a good university, get a job, get married, start a family and then we die, back to the earth in which we came from. I feel like a sheep. I feel like I’ve been this consuming machine. I’ve shaped myself to be this perfect little girl, this perfect civilian. I go to school. I study. I got a 9 to 5. But now what? Is this all that my life will amount to? Do all my struggles end with me staring at a computer screen for 8 hours until the day that I die?

That is my existential dread. I am a sheep with no agency. So this is the start of my journey. I’ve waken up and chosen the red pill. I want to pave my own path. I want to be a force to be reckoned with. I want it all.
